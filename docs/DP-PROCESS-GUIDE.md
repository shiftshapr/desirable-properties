# DP Workgroups: Principles, Protocols & Practices to Nov 13 (V1)

As of 6 October 2026. Canonical page: https://desirableproperties.org/process. Readiness board: https://desirableproperties.org/v1-readiness. Deepi reads this file as reference; keep it in step with `challenge-site/src/data/dp-process.ts`.

## Purpose and timeline

Every DP workgroup takes its chapter from the open working draft (Version 0.77) to Version 1.0 by Friday 13 November 2026, 11:30 PT. The editorial freeze is Monday 9 November at 1 p.m. PT; after it only verified corrections and acknowledgments change. Version 1.0 is published with a permanent Ordinal edition as part of the Digital Monument, alongside the second edition of The Metaweb.

Weekly Monday sessions, 11:30 PT, each with one job:

| Date | Session | The week's job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 Oct | Turn Feedback into Proposals | Turn chapter comments into specific proposals with owners and reviewers |
| 12 Oct | Resolve the Hard Questions | Settle competing wording, missing evidence and substantive objections |
| 19 Oct | Bring the Chapters Together | Integrate reviewed changes into coherent chapter drafts |
| 26 Oct | Review Across Workgroups | Check terminology, overlaps, conflicts and omissions across chapters |
| 2 Nov | V1 Readiness Review | Review each chapter against the checklist; name release blockers |
| 9 Nov | Final Review Before Launch | Verify final corrections and acknowledgments before the 1 p.m. freeze |
| 13 Nov | Desirable Properties V1 Launch | Present Version 1.0, recognize contributors, discuss the next phase |

## Principles

1. Synthesis, not stacking. A chapter integrates ideas once, in their best home; overlapping proposals are reconciled into one passage.
2. Every contribution is traceable. Each change keeps its contributors' names and its source (Canopi post, Gov Hub proposal, CFI submission).
3. Anchored to text. Proposals point at a specific passage and say exactly what changes.
4. Decisions carry reasons. Every merge, decline or "considered" carries a review note.
5. Two people agree before the book changes. No single person, including coordinators and admins, incorporates a proposal into a DP alone.
6. AI assists; people decide. Deepi, Hermes and Astra draft, reconcile and check; a person approves every change, and AI-drafted text is labelled.
7. Normative and testable. MUST, SHOULD and MAY are used deliberately; an untestable requirement becomes guidance. Version 1.0 feeds the ML-REQs in December.

## Protocols (how a change reaches Version 1.0)

1. Propose on the book: Discuss, Patch or Insert on book.desirableproperties.org on the exact passage, or Gov Hub's patch form.
2. Filing is automatic: every 30 minutes Canopi patches and inserts are filed into Gov Hub as pending proposals under their authors. Posts already decided by an Astra pass, and Astra's reconciled posts, are not filed again.
3. Astra passes reconcile batches into one change per idea, crediting every source, posted to Canopi once.
4. Two co-editors decide: a proposal is incorporated when both co-editors approve. Either co-editor, or the coordinator, can decline or mark it considered. Every decision carries a review note.
5. Nothing waits silently (proposed): a proposal pending more than 7 days goes on the next Monday session's agenda.
6. Publish as a numbered revision: the coordinator or a co-editor publishes the working revision as the next ML-Draft; the live chapter follows it.
7. Freeze: after 9 November, 1 p.m. PT, only verified corrections and acknowledgments change.

Proposed cut-off for new proposals entering Version 1.0: 19 October (Bring the Chapters Together).

## Roles

- Member (3 or more per workgroup): proposes, comments, reviews, takes practice tasks. Join the workgroup.
- Co-editor (exactly 2 per DP): approves (both needed) or declines proposals; publishes revisions. Claim an open seat on the workgroup page: signed in, account at least 7 days old, a short statement, at most 2 DPs per person. The coordinator can revoke within 7 days and a super admin at any time, always with a reason.
- Coordinator (1): runs the weekly rhythm, declines or marks considered, publishes revisions; cannot incorporate alone. Nominated, approved by a layer admin.

Co-lead, Editor, Presenter, Facilitator, Liaison and Recorder are retired roles.

## V1 readiness checklist (per DP)

Eight items are checked automatically from Gov Hub and Canopi data; four are ticked by a co-editor or the coordinator.

1. Workgroup has at least 3 members (auto, 12 Oct)
2. Both co-editor seats filled (auto, 12 Oct)
3. Coordinator in place (auto, 12 Oct)
4. Every Canopi patch and insert on the chapter is filed in Gov Hub or decided by Astra (auto, 19 Oct)
5. No proposal pending longer than 7 days (auto, 26 Oct)
6. Every declined or considered proposal has a review note (auto, 26 Oct)
7. Integrated changes published as a numbered ML-Draft revision (auto, 2 Nov)
8. Chapter checked against neighbouring DPs for terms, overlaps and conflicts (manual, 26 Oct)
9. Every MUST and SHOULD is testable or rephrased as guidance (manual, 2 Nov)
10. All contributors acknowledged (manual, 2 Nov)
11. No release blockers open from the readiness review (manual, 9 Nov)
12. V1 candidate revision published before the freeze (auto, 9 Nov 1 p.m. PT)
