# DP1 – Federated Authentication & Accountability

## Introduction

Trust on the open web depends on verifiable identity and accountable action. A meta-layer must support federated authentication that lets participants assert identity across sites while keeping credential sovereignty with the individual, not a single platform gatekeeper.

## Core requirements

Participants should be able to authenticate across communities while retaining agency over their credentials. Accountability mechanisms must bind actions to identities in ways that communities can audit, challenge, and remediate through governed processes.
