Our practice rule

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your personal information for advertising targeting or to build advertising profiles.
  • We do not share your personal information with third parties for their marketing.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to run the services:

  • Account and contact information – such as name, email, username, and identifiers from sign-in providers (for example Google or other SSO tools we enable).
  • Content you submit – patches, inserts, comments, uploads, forms, and messages you send us.
  • Technical and usage data – IP address, device and browser type, approximate location derived from IP, pages or features used, and security or error logs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies – needed to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and secure the service. We do not use advertising cookies.

We do not require government ID numbers, full bank account numbers, or similar sensitive identifiers to use these services. Please do not paste them into forms. If you do, we may delete them.

Children. These services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. (Youth products such as Pacha’s Pajamas, if offered, will use a separate notice.)

How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, secure, and improve the services
  • Authenticate accounts and prevent abuse
  • Process and, where applicable, publish contributions under our Terms
  • Respond to you and to legal requests
  • Measure basic product performance (not for ads)

We do not use personal information for sale or advertising targeting.

Who we share with

We share personal information only with:

  • Service providers that host infrastructure, authentication, email, and security tooling, under obligations to use the data only for our instructions
  • Advisors (for example accounting) under confidentiality, when needed
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • A successor if we reorganize or transfer assets, with continued protections

We do not share personal information with advertising networks.

Retention

We keep account and submission data while your account is active and as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or de-identify it when we no longer need it, unless law requires a longer period. Security logs are kept for a limited time.
