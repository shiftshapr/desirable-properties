What we collect

We collect only what we need to run the services:

  • Account and contact information – such as name, email, username, and identifiers from sign-in providers (for example Google or other SSO tools we enable).
  • Content you submit – patches, inserts, comments, uploads, forms, and messages you send us.
  • Technical and usage data – IP address, device and browser type, approximate location derived from IP, pages or features used, and security or error logs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies – needed to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and secure the service. We do not use advertising cookies.

We do not require government ID numbers, full bank account numbers, or similar sensitive identifiers to use these services. Please do not paste them into forms. If you do, we may delete them.

We do not share personal information with advertising networks.

SMS opt-in data, text message alerts and your phone number

Adding a phone number is optional. If you add one to get Desirable Properties and Canopi alerts by text message (SMS), WhatsApp or Signal, we store:

  • your phone number,
  • the time you confirmed it with a one-time code,
  • the line type (for example mobile or VoIP), country and carrier name, from a number lookup,
  • which messages you turned on (alerts, announcements; text, WhatsApp, Signal) and when,
  • which version of the consent wording you agreed to.

Why. We use this to send the messages you asked for, to confirm the number is real and belongs to you, and to keep one confirmed number per account. We send announcements only if you ticked the separate box for them. We do not use your number for advertising targeting.

Who can see it. Only you can see your number in your account, shown masked except the last 4 digits. It is never shown on public pages or to other users. Our administrators can see it only to run and support the service.

Removing it. When you remove your number in your account settings, we delete the number and all of the details above, and all messages stop. Replying STOP turns off text messages.

Twilio. We use Twilio as our service provider to deliver text and WhatsApp messages, to look up line type and carrier, and to send and check one-time codes. Twilio processes your number only to provide these services to us.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We do not share or sell your mobile number or text message opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text message opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except service providers like Twilio that deliver the messages for us.
