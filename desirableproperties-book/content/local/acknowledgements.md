# Acknowledgements

This book exists because many people chose to treat the Meta-Layer not as a finished product but as a shared inquiry – one worth sustaining across meetings, drafts, inscriptions, and open challenge rounds.

We are deeply grateful to Dr. Suzanne Sincavage and the Foundation for Biodefense Research for their crucial support of the Meta-Layer Initiative to ensure the forward progress of this work.

These acknowledgements, like the rest of this book, are preserved as enduring digital artifacts – inscribed on Bitcoin and intended to live onchain, or through whatever preservation methods may follow – so that future generations can see who supported this work and who helped bring it into being.

## Contributors

Contributors to *The Layered Web: The Desirable Properties of a Meta-Layer* are listed below in order of contribution – honoring those whose ideas, critiques, implementations, and stewardship arrived earliest in the collective arc of this work. Rather than a ranking of authority, this is a record of how the book came to be. The list will grow as new contributors join the Desirable Properties Challenge and as earlier work is reconciled with the permanent record.

1. Vint Cerf – At the Meta-Layer Initiative kickoff meeting on September 16, 2024, Vint Cerf suggested that the community begin by defining the *desirable properties* of a Meta-Layer. He also recommended that we start off with federated strong authentication. That framing launched the Calls for Input, the satplication synthesis, and the open edition you are reading now.
