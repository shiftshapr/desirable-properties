# Edition and license

*The Layered Web: The Desirable Properties of a Meta-Layer*

**Version 1.0 · November 13, 2026**

With a foreword by Vint Cerf. Published by the Meta-Layer Initiative.

Developed in the open by the Meta-Layer community through two Calls for Input (2024 and 2025), the Desirable Properties Challenge and its workgroups, and approved by the Version 1.0 Steering Committee: 1,635 proposals decided, and all 28 documents signed off by two steering members.
