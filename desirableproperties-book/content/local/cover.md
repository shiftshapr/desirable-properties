# The Layered Web

*The Desirable Properties of a Meta-Layer*

![The Layered Web book cover — twenty-three Desirable Properties for the Meta-Layer](/assets/cover.png)

This is open-access 0.77 edition of the forthcoming book, *The Layered Web: The Desirable Properties of a Meta-Layer*. It contains the twenty-three Desirable Properties of a Meta-Layer – community-defined foundations for decentralized digital infrastructure. Most chapters are inscribed on Bitcoin; working drafts and community patches live on [Gov Hub](https://hub.themetalayer.org/doc/all/?collection=desirable-properties) and the Canopi overlay.
